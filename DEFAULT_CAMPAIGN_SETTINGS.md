# Default Campaign - What's Included

When you create your business profile, a **Default Campaign** is automatically created for you with pre-configured settings to get you started quickly.

## Auto-Populated Settings

### **Sender Name**
- **Value**: Your business name (from your business profile)
- **Example**: "Acme Corporation"
- **What it means**: This personalizes the "From" field that recipients see
- **Display**: Appears as `"Acme Corporation" via Monumentum <hello@monumentum.com>`

### **Reply-To Email**
- **Value**: Your business email (if set) OR your signup email
- **Example**: "john@acme.com"
- **What it means**: When prospects reply, it goes directly to YOUR inbox
- **Important**: This is where you'll receive all responses!
- **Note**: Emails are sent from Monumentum's infrastructure (hello@monumentum.com), but replies come to you

### **Target Audience (Pre-configured)**
- **Industries**: All
- **Company Size**: 50 - 10,000 employees
- **Job Titles**:
  - VP of HR
  - Chief People Officer
  - Head of HR
  - VP of Product
  - Head of Product
  - VP of Finance
  - Head of Finance
  - Chief Financial Officer

### **Optional Settings (Not Set)**
- **Calendar Link**: None (you can add your Calendly/Cal.com link)
- **Email Signature**: None (you can add a custom signature)

## How to Use the Default Campaign

The Default Campaign is perfect for:
1. **Quick CSV uploads** - Upload a prospect list without creating a campaign first
2. **Testing** - Try out the system with pre-configured settings
3. **Getting started** - See how campaigns work before creating custom ones

## Customizing Your Default Campaign

You can edit the Default Campaign at any time:
1. Go to **Campaign Management**
2. Click the **Edit** button on "Default Campaign"
3. Modify any settings:
   - Change target audience criteria
   - Add your calendar link
   - Add an email signature
   - Update sender information if needed

## Important Notes

✅ **Replies Come to You**: All prospect replies go directly to your reply-to email (your business email or signup email)

✅ **Ready to Use**: The Default Campaign is immediately usable - upload prospects via CSV and start generating personalized emails

✅ **Flexible**: Edit the settings anytime to match your outreach strategy

⚠️ **Status**: The campaign starts as "Draft" - you can activate it when ready to send emails

## Email Sending (SaaS Model)

When emails are sent from your campaigns:

```text
From: "Your Business Name" via Monumentum <hello@monumentum.com>
Reply-To: your@email.com
```

This SaaS approach ensures:
- **Zero Setup**: No AWS, SES, or DNS configuration required
- **Professional Appearance**: Branded sender builds trust with recipients
- **Monumentum Manages Deliverability**: We handle email reputation, compliance, and infrastructure
- **Direct Replies**: Prospect responses go straight to your inbox
- **Instant Activation**: Start sending emails immediately after signup
- **Reliable Infrastructure**: Built on proven AWS SES with proper warm-up and monitoring
